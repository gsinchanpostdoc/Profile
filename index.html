Research & stakeholder tools

Models you can open

Decision-support applications built from the research — for foresters, wildlife managers, and game planners.

Scandinavian Species Distribution Models

Habitat density for moose, roe deer and grey wolf across 374 grid cells under five IPCC pathways, 2025–2060 — machine-learning SDMs coupled to predator–prey ODEs.

ML SDMs · ODE · CMIP6 · Scandinavia
Open the model
Forage Energy Meta-Database

Metabolizable energy and Jacobs' D preference indices for boreal ungulates — 72 records, Menke coefficients, open to community contribution.

Bioenergetics · meta-analysis · open data
Explore the database
AlderIPM-Sim

A simulator of the black alder eco-epidemic system: within-season ODE and annual map, parasitoid biocontrol, regime-shift early warnings, and integrated pest-management comparison.

Eco-epidemic ODE · optimal control · IPM
Run the simulator
